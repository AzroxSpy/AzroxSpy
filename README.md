## `// WHO WE ARE`

We are **AzroxSpy**, an anonymous independent collective focused on cybersecurity, digital privacy, technology research and open-source development.

We believe in knowledge, transparency, responsible research and digital freedom.

Our objective is to understand technology, explore security, develop useful tools and contribute to a safer digital environment.

> Privacy is not a crime. Knowledge is not a weapon. Responsibility matters.
