## `01` // ABOUT

> Independent minds. Shared knowledge. Responsible research.

AzroxSpy is an independent collective focused on cybersecurity,
technology, digital privacy, open-source development and research.

Our objective is to explore technology, understand digital systems,
develop useful tools and contribute to a more informed digital world.

We believe in knowledge, transparency and responsible innovation.
